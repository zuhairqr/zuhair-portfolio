Workshop, spare parts and company details
Workshop management

Service businesses
Auto, motorcycle, marine, truck, and heavy equipment repair shops, plus detailing, wrapping, tire, and battery centers.
B2B spare parts trade
The VinZone network connects parts sellers, workshops, and fleets in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt. Buyers can search live stock by part number or VIN, confirm fit, get quotes, and track orders. Sellers can manage purchase orders and sync catalogs with TecDoc.
